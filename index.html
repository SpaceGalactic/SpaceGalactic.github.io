Galactic Brianne

A personal AI desktop companion made by Galactic Nugs for his own Windows PC.

Galactic Brianne is a 3D character who lives on one person's desktop. She chats by voice, sets reminders, controls music and lights, helps with personal projects, and, when her owner asks, reads his calendar and email and finds DJ sets on YouTube.

It is not a public service: it isn't offered to anyone else, has no user accounts, and has no servers. Everything runs on the owner's own computer.
