Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Galactic Brianne is a personal desktop application used only by its creator, Galactic Nugs, on his own Windows PC. It is not offered to the public and has no servers, accounts or analytics.

Google data it uses

When the owner connects his Google account, Galactic Brianne may access, only when he asks:

  • Google Calendar: to read upcoming events and, after he confirms, add events.
  • Gmail: to read recent message headers and short snippets, and to save reply drafts. It never sends email.
  • YouTube: to search public videos (for example DJ sets).

How that data is handled

  • Data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's PC and is not stored on any server.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in Windows Credential Manager on that PC. Passwords are never seen or stored.
  • Email and calendar content is processed by an AI model running locally on the PC. It is only sent to an outside AI service (Anthropic's Claude) if the owner explicitly turns that option on, and only for the request he made.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else, and never used to train AI models.
  • The owner can disconnect at any time in the app's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which revokes access immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Galactic Brianne's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

The app can also connect to Spotify, Discord and Govee lights at the owner's request, using sign-ins he provides. Those connections are used only to carry out his requests and follow the same rules: no selling, no sharing, nothing stored off his PC.
